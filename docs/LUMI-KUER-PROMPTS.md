## speed

Datei: `assets/ice-skater-speed-v2.webp`

Use case: stylized-concept. Production animation asset for a transparent website character. Input image 1 is an IDENTITY AND COSTUME reference only. Keep exactly Lumi: the same adult athletic slender brunette female skater, neat brown bun with silver hair clip, warm expressive face, pale ice-blue long-sleeve fitted skating dress with delicate silver embroidered bodice and short lavender skirt, tan tights, white figure skates with narrow steel blades. Soft detailed dimensional painted cartoon style. Create a perfectly regular 4 by 4 sprite atlas with exactly SIXTEEN equal square cells, read left to right then top to bottom. Each cell contains ONE COMPLETE fully drawn body including both hands, head and both skates, never body-part cutouts. Same camera distance and anatomical scale in every cell, consistent lighting. All skates' support baseline at 92% of each cell height; head height can change naturally when the knees bend. Leave generous empty cell margins so no part touches neighboring cells. Real alpha transparency outside the character. No backdrop, no ice surface, no ground plane, no shadows, no reflections, no grid, no text, no numbers, no motion trails. Do not reproduce any extraction noise or colored edge artifacts from the reference. Action: a seamless energetic speed-building figure skating stroke cycle toward screen right, the torso always facing the same three-quarter right perspective (NO alternating mirrored shoulders). The skater is visibly LOW in her knees, hips lowered, torso inclined forwards gracefully about 25 degrees, neck lifted, strong pushing legs extending backward to drive speed. Soft counter-swing of BOTH arms from behind hips to low rounded in front, subtle continuous weight transfer. Frames 0-3 compress the skating knee and sweep one leg behind; 4-7 recover the free skate under the hips with small crossing step; 8-11 repeat a strong push with the other leg; 12-15 recover and prepare to repeat frame0. Deep athletic knee bend throughout, not running, not jumping, not a stationary upright stance. Full, smoothly varying action frames. All sixteen frames must be distinct sequential phases of ONE flowing low crouched skating cycle.
