# Weiße Glas-Eislandschaft

Originale, für diese Demo erzeugte PNG: `assets/glass-ice-scene-v1.png`.

- Größe: 2172 × 724 Pixel (3:1), ohne Transparenz.
- Erstellt: 24.11.2026.
- Gestaltung: weiße, durchgehende Eisfläche in feiner Glasoptik, helle minimale Winterkulisse. Keine eingebettete Figur. Kein ovales Becken und keine Umrandung.
- Darstellung: über die volle Seitenbreite, mit `object-fit: cover` und mittigem Ausschnitt auf schmalen Displays. Die vorhandene schwebende Figur bleibt unabhängig davon aktiv.
- Die PNG liegt unverändert in `assets` und wird vom bestehenden Seiten-Build mit veröffentlicht.

## Verwendeter Bildprompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: an original full-bleed PNG panorama replacing a website footer scene. Create a contemporary, exceptionally refined minimalist white glass ice landscape. Very wide horizontal panoramic composition, approximately 3:1. The lower two thirds of the entire picture are ONE CONTINUOUS smooth white translucent frozen surface that extends completely beyond the left, right and bottom edges of the frame. The ice must meet BOTH side edges at all heights in the foreground: absolutely no oval rink, ellipse, island, pedestal, tabletop, border, rounded card or surrounding moat. Pale pearlescent white frosted glass with extremely fine cool-silver hairline ice traces, subtle clear reflections and delicate icy blue refraction; glass feels flat and expansive, not chunky. The distant horizon is a very soft sculptural white snowscape with two or three barely visible thin translucent ice forms placed far in the background, a hint of pale mist, generous quiet negative space and soft diffuse daylight. Almost entirely white and warm ivory with the faintest silver, pale blue and mint accents. Elegant premium modern architectural CGI, delicate fine details and crisp high-quality rendering, serene and slightly enchanted without glitter clutter. Light almost-white sky fades gently into the horizon. All background scenery stays subtle and distant, while the uninterrupted glass ice floor dominates the panorama. No people, no avatars, no skaters, no animals, no forests, no dark night sky, no moon, no text, no lettering, no logos, no UI, no frame. Opaque white background, not transparency. The scene itself must fill the entire image edge to edge.
